Xuxin Tang

Human-AI Interaction Researcher

(How to pronouce my first name: “Sheshin”.)

Hello! 👋 I’m a Computer Science PhD student at Virginia Tech’s Sanghani Center for Artificial Intelligence & Data Analytics, advised by Prof. Chris North. I design and implement human-AI collaborative systems using state-of-the-art LLM and agentic frameworks. I bridge the gap between AI reasoning and human oversight by architecting intuitive visual interfaces that prioritize system transparency, generalizability, and operational efficiency. My unique background in large-scale recommendation systems allows me to transition abstract cognitive frameworks into reliable, real-world applications.

My research explores the intersection of human-AI interaction and spatial computing, using spatial environments like Miro to establish a common ground for collaborative sensemaking. What sets my work apart is a rare combination of rigorous HCI methodologies (sensemaking frameworks, spatial cognition, user studies, visual analytics) and robust technical execution (data analytics, scalable AI pipelines, and production software development). I don’t just design human-centered concepts—I build high-performance interactive architectures from the ground up and empirically evaluate their cognitive impact.

Before joining Virginia Tech, I spent nearly three years in industry, starting as a full-stack development engineer before advancing to machine learning engineering where I worked on large-scale recommendation systems. I hold both B.Eng. and M.S. degrees from Wuhan University.

I am actively seeking full-time Postdoctoral Researcher positions focusing on human-AI interaction and agentic systems. Please feel free to reach out if you are interested in my background and research!
